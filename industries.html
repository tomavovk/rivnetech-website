Manufacturing

Manufacturing

Production runs on the floor — but the office is always a step behind, chasing status and reacting to surprises.

Challenges
  • Production status invisible to office and management
  • Supplier delays surface too late to reroute or reschedule
  • QC results disconnected from job records
  • Scheduling still lives in spreadsheets or whiteboards
Typically built
  • Production scheduling board with capacity view
  • Shop-floor status tracker linked to ERP orders
  • Vendor & component arrival tracking
  • Quality inspection log per job and product
Distribution

Distribution

Orders move through too many systems — by the time something goes wrong, it's already a customer problem.

Challenges
  • Order status spread across warehouse, ERP, and email
  • Shipping exceptions caught late with no structured response
  • No real-time inventory signal across locations
  • Pick and pack errors invisible until a complaint arrives
Typically built
  • Order visibility dashboard from receipt to shipment
  • Exception & on-hold workflows with owner assignment
  • Dispatch coordination and route-readiness checks
  • Customer-facing order status portal
Field Service / Install

Field Service & Install

The office and field crews work from different lists — gaps between them create missed jobs and late completions.

Challenges
  • Office and field operate from separate schedules
  • Job status not updated until techs are back in the office
  • Install completion unconfirmed for days after the visit
  • Techs have no mobile access to job details or history
Typically built
  • Dispatch calendar with crew and vehicle assignments
  • Mobile job-completion forms with photo or signature
  • Site-readiness checklists before dispatch
  • Customer approval portal for sign-off and revisions
Job Shops

Job Shops & Custom Fabrication

Every job is different — which makes standard ERP tracking a poor fit and custom visibility nearly impossible.

Challenges
  • Unique jobs make standardised tracking hard to maintain
  • Customer approvals stall production with no audit trail
  • Material traceability across custom runs is manual
  • Quote-to-delivery visibility gaps leave reps guessing
Typically built
  • Custom job tracker from quote through delivery
  • Customer approval & revision portal with version history
  • Vendor & component tracking per job
  • Shop-floor status board linked to live job list
