Scheduling

Production Scheduling & Capacity Planning

Shop floor has no single view of what's queued — priorities shift by email and whiteboard.

Always know what's on the floor and what's next.

  • Visual schedule board by machine, line, or crew
  • Real-time capacity load per work centre
  • Drag-and-drop job resequencing
  • Conflict and overload alerts
  • ERP order sync — no double entry

Works well when you have multiple work centres competing for the same resources and capacity changes day to day.

Visibility

Order Visibility & Status Management

Customers and reps chase order status by phone and email, pulling teams away from real work.

Real-time status accessible to everyone who needs it.

  • Order timeline from quote to delivery
  • Milestone-based status flags and colour coding
  • Automated customer-facing status updates
  • Rep and manager dashboards in one view
  • Filter and search across all live orders

Works well when you handle long-lead or multi-step production cycles where customers expect proactive communication.

Vendor Tracking

Vendor / Component Tracking

Supplier delays are invisible until the line stops — teams find out too late to react.

Catch delays before they reach the production line.

  • Component arrival status per order and PO
  • Lead time tracking with expected vs. actual
  • Supplier delay alerts with downstream impact
  • Linked to production schedule — see affected jobs
  • Vendor performance history at a glance

Works well when you depend on third-party components with variable lead times and a single delay can stall multiple orders.

Exceptions

Exceptions & On-Hold Workflows

Problem orders sit in limbo with no clear owner — resolution happens through long email chains.

Every exception has an owner and a resolution path.

  • Structured hold queue with reason codes
  • Auto-assignment rules by exception type
  • Escalation timers and reminders
  • Full resolution audit trail per order
  • Real-time hold count visible to management

Works well when issues regularly stall production and there's no structured path from flagging a problem to closing it.

Approvals

Customer Approvals / Portals

Approval loops happen over email with no audit trail — it's unclear what was approved, when, and by whom.

Faster sign-offs with full traceability baked in.

  • Customer-facing portal with order details and attachments
  • Approval gates that block production until sign-off
  • Timestamped approval history on every order
  • Revision request flow with version tracking
  • Automated reminders for pending approvals

Works well when your orders require customer or internal sign-off before production, and delays in approval cause downstream bottlenecks.

Dispatch

Dispatch / Install Coordination

Field teams and the office work from different lists — schedules clash and jobs fall through the gaps.

One source of truth from factory floor to final site.

  • Dispatch calendar with crew and vehicle assignments
  • Job status updates from field to office in real time
  • Site readiness checklist before dispatch
  • Linked to production status — dispatch only when ready
  • Install completion confirmation with photo or signature

Works well when you coordinate delivery and installation as part of the order, with field teams who need mobile access.

Exceptions

Quality / Inspection Tracking

QC results live in disconnected spreadsheets — defects are caught late and traceability is weak.

Quality data linked to every job, not stuck in a file.

  • Inspection checklists per product type or stage
  • Pass / fail logging with defect categorisation
  • Automatic hold trigger on failed inspection
  • Defect trends and repeat-failure reports
  • Full QC history per order and per product

Works well when you have recurring inspection points across production and need defect data linked back to specific jobs and customers.

Visibility

Operations Dashboards & Alerts

Managers make decisions on yesterday's data — nothing surfaces a problem until it's urgent.

Live operational pulse with smart alerts that surface what matters.

  • Live order and production status overview
  • Configurable alert rules — overdue, on-hold, delayed
  • Role-based views for ops, sales, and management
  • Trend charts: throughput, hold rate, on-time delivery
  • No data exports needed — refreshes automatically

Works well when you need cross-department visibility without replacing your ERP, and different teams need different slices of the same data.
